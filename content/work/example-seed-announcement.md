Most seed announcements are a tweet, a logo wall, and a blog post nobody
finishes. The round is the most attention a company gets for free all year, and
it usually gets spent on a graphic made in an afternoon.

## What we made

One shoot day in the office, no studio, no crew of eight. The founder talking
about the problem rather than the raise, because the raise is the least
interesting thing about the week it happens.

- A two minute film for the blog and the press follow up
- Four vertical cuts, each one a single idea from the interview
- A LinkedIn post in the founder's voice, written from the same transcript
- Motion titles and lower thirds built from the existing brand kit

## How it went

The full film carried the announcement on the company blog. The vertical cuts
went out over the following ten days, which kept the round in people's feeds
long after the news itself stopped being news. The written post came from the
same interview transcript, so the founder never had to sit down and write it.

> The point of a round announcement is not the number. It is the one week a year
> when strangers will listen to you explain what you are building.

## What we would do again

Shoot the interview before anyone writes a script. Everything useful in this
project came out of the founder answering a question badly the first time and
well the third.
